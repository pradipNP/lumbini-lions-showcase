🏆 CHAMPIONS

From bottom of the table in Season 1 to lifting the trophy at TU Cricket Ground, Kirtipur — the greatest turnaround in Nepal Premier League history.

FINAL
Champions Lumbini Lions 86/4 9 overs · Won by 6 wickets
01

The Collapse

Sudurpaschim bowled out for 85 — the lowest final total in NPL history. Ruben Trumpelmann and Sher Malla took 3 wickets each.

02

The Hat-trick

Captain Rohit Paudel's hat-trick in the 11th over — 12 runs from his spell. Leadership when it mattered most.

03

Four Sixes. One Over.

Dinesh Adhikari exploded with 42 off 13 balls — four sixes in the opening over of the chase. Pure Red Paltan fury.

04

Six Straight Wins

From crisis to coronation — six consecutive victories, equalling the season's longest winning streak.

05

Fireworks at Kirtipur

Dilip Nath sealed the winning run. Maiden NPL title. Lumbini Lions — champions of Nepal.
